📐 Big-O, without the maths panic

Big-O is not about how many seconds something takes. It answers one question: when the input gets bigger, how much worse does this get?

That's it. A fast algorithm on 10 items tells you nothing. The question is what happens at 10,000.

🎓 Real-life example: Finding one student in a class of 60 is easy however you do it. Now find one student in a college of 60,000. The method that made no difference before is suddenly the difference between a minute and a whole day. Big-O describes exactly that gap — and it ignores small stuff like how fast your laptop is, because that never changes the shape of the curve.
